About Parmarth

Your path to degree completion.

Life doesn't always follow the regular school timetable. Illness, work, family responsibility, or years spent training for sport can mean school gets interrupted — but that shouldn't mean the door to a certificate or degree closes.

Parmarth Education Counselling exists for exactly this. We guide students and working adults through NIOS, distance education, and alternate board options — explaining what's actually possible for your situation, honestly, before any form is filled.
